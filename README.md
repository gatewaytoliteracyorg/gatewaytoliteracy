# Gateway Bridge

Build a website using this prompt: System / Style Persona & Branding: Act as a professional web designer building a modern, clean, and inspiring website for Gateway to Literacy, modeled closely after the layout, tone, and visual design of Building Financial Futures of America.

Use our official brand identity based on the Gateway to Literacy logo (featuring the white bridge supported by three distinct pillars):

Background / Off-White: #FBFEF9[cite: 1]

Primary Text / Dark Background: #191923[cite: 1]

Bright Beginnings Bundles Accent (Blue): #0E79B2[cite: 1]

Health Literacy Accent (Magenta/Red): #BF1363[cite: 1]

Financial Literacy Accent (Orange): #F39237[cite: 1]

Design & Layout Guidelines:

Header & Navigation Bar: Include the Gateway to Literacy logo prominently in the navigation bar alongside links for: Home, About Us, Programs (Dropdown or sub-links: BBB, Health Literacy, Financial Literacy), Partner Schools, Impact, Donate, and Contact Us. Add a prominent "Donate" CTA button highlighted in #BF1363[cite: 1].

Hero Section: High-impact headline about empowering elementary students through educational outreach, backed by three core pillars (BBB, Health Literacy, and Financial Literacy). Feature the logo's bridge icon and metric highlights with clear CTA buttons ("Our Impact", "Donate via Zeffy").

About Us Section: Highlight our background and key national recognition: Placed 2nd Nationally at the Future Business Leaders of America (FBLA) National Leadership Conference in the Community Service Presentation event for our work with Building Bright Futures (BBB). Include a mission statement focused on bridging educational equity across local elementary schools.

Dedicated Program Pages Structure:

Bright Beginnings Bundles Page (Color Theme: #0E79B2)[cite: 1]

Header Accent & Hero: #0E79B2 background accents[cite: 1] with the title Bright Beginnings Bundles (BBB).

Program Overview: Focuses on early childhood literacy and essential learning kits distributed directly to elementary students.

Key Components:

Custom curriculum workbooks and age-appropriate reading books.

Essential school supplies and hands-on learning kits tailored to low-income elementary students.

Interactive classroom workshops and reading sessions led by student volunteers.

Recognitions & Accomplishments: Callout section highlighting the 2nd Place FBLA National Award in Community Service for the BBB implementation.

Impact Metrics: Number of bundles delivered, schools served, and reading engagement gains.

Health Literacy Workshops Page (Color Theme: #BF1363)[cite: 1]

Header Accent & Hero: #BF1363 background accents[cite: 1] with the title Health & Wellness Literacy.

Program Overview: Educates elementary students on physical health, mental well-being, nutrition, and hygiene to build lifelong healthy habits.

Key Components:

Interactive workshops covering basic anatomy, balanced nutrition, active lifestyle choices, and germ prevention.

Take-home health kits including personal hygiene essentials and fun activity workbooks.

Engaging group activities, storytelling, and visual demonstrations designed for young learners.

Impact Metrics: Workshops delivered, hygiene kits distributed, and student survey feedback.

Financial Literacy Workshops Page (Color Theme: #F39237)[cite: 1]

Header Accent & Hero: #F39237 background accents[cite: 1] with the title Financial Literacy & Empowerment.

Program Overview: Introduces elementary students to fundamental economic concepts, money management, saving strategies, and smart spending.

Key Components:

Hands-on activities on the concepts of Earn, Save, Spend, and Give.

Interactive budgeting simulations, coin/cash identification games, and basic entrepreneurship workshops.

Student piggy bank distribution and family financial guidebooks.

Impact Metrics: Participating classrooms, financial workbooks completed, and math/financial proficiency improvements.

Donate Page (Zeffy Integration): Dedicated "Donate" page styled to match the site's design. Include an embedded Zeffy donation form container (or placeholder for Zeffy iframe integration) so donors can contribute 100% platform-fee-free. Add a brief message detailing how donations directly support school outreach supplies, student workbooks, and program expansion across all three pillars.

School Tracking & Outreach Dashboard (Interactive Section): Create a clean, filterable data table and visual summary cards displaying our current outreach status across target schools. Color-code service badges using #0E79B2 for BBB initiatives[cite: 1], #BF1363 for Health Literacy[cite: 1], and #F39237 for Financial Literacy[cite: 1].
